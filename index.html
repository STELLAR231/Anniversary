The three year census

3rd Atlantis
Anniversary

It's been three years since this place started. Kinda crazy to think about. When Yoshi started this place, he wasn't really trying to grow the place until others like Jina, Sovi, Darky and other names he told me, which I forgot, helped him out. It wasn't until November, one or two months after the site was created, that this place got any activity. Now, of course, it's in the state as we know it. But give credit where its due: all the admins and mod team of that time, Yoshi, Anish, users like RF, Jia, Authy, and a lot more names even I don't know, put in a lot of effort into making however many mcs that you see, which is nothing to sneeze at. Later, many more key users joined, and whether you liked them or not, they too, at one point, prolly contributed to the place that this once was.

I would usually write a yap, but no one wants to read allat, so here's a report of the three years, into one. Just statistics I compiled using python scripts over the disqus database. Big names like RF, Authy, Goat, Ryan, Cof, Quin, Shadow and more are missing because they deleted their accounts.

Some tables are expandable and collapsible. You can scroll the tables by dragging your fingers left and right. And there's a comment section at the bottom.
